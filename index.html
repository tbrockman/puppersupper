Analysis per the AAFCO adult-maintenance profile

CheckRangeStatus

Most nutrients have no AAFCO maximum: a “–” in the maximum’s place marks an open-ended range, and being well above a minimum is normal for complete foods. Where another published upper figure exists (EU legal limits, NRC, FEDIAF) it appears as an underlined number; hover it for the reasoning and source, and a diet above it reads “above advisory level”. When the calories are short, most nutrients will be too: feed more before adding supplements. This is a planning tool, not a veterinary formulation.
